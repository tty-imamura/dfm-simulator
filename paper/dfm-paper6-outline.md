# Paper 6 (outline, v0.1 — wave 248): *Calibration, Numerical Controls, and Limits of a Dragging-Field Toy Model*

Status: **outline only** (no manuscript yet). Language of the manuscript: EN + JA, same build pipeline as papers 1–5 once a `.tex` exists.
Scope: the negative results and numerical controls accumulated in waves 220–248 on the DFM simulator. This paper makes **no positive
claim** about real gravitomagnetism; it records what a 2D dragging-field toy model can and cannot reproduce, and how each verdict was fixed.

## 0. Abstract (draft sentence)
A 2D dragging-field toy model (DFM) with one frame field u, a pull-weighted transport law (E6′) and an inertia law
f = 1 + k_F(αχ_A + βχ_B) is assessed with an explicit parameter-and-data ledger. Earth–Moon and Saturn calibrations,
the J0737 calibration example, and conditional compact-binary hold-outs are distinguished. We report ten negative results within the tested model family, with numerical controls (step-size convergence,
Float32/Float64 drag-field precision, detector method), and state the limits of the model as verdicts fixed to one line each.

## 1. Model summary (what is actually integrated)
- Frame field u, pull weight (m/d²) with D0pull, E6′ transport (first-order in time), pairReduced.
- Inertia law f = 1 + k_F(αχ_A + βχ_B); f ≈ 2 at χ saturation.
- Spin–spin toy `physics.spinSpin` (U = λ (G/c²) Q₁Q₂/ρ³); observed-Q transcription `body.spinDipole`.
- Core v2 (Mc/J primary variables, contraction with budget gate, shed, bare-core terminal state).
- Numerical controls: `framePrecision:"double"`, half-kick split, dt ladders, two precession detectors.

## 2. Calibration set and hold-outs (declared, not inferred)
- Fit: 🌘 Earth–Moon (D0pull, 8.85-yr apsidal cycle), 🪨 Mercury, 💿 Saturn ring (frameSource), ⚡ J0737 (f only).
- Conditional hold-outs: PSR J1757−1854 / J1946+2052 (GR-derived input masses and unknown companion spins must be declared; after a law is chosen on these three systems they become a development set and a fresh frozen hold-out is required). GW150914's +1.0% eccentricity offset is relative to an adopted central value, not a rejection at observational confidence; retain it as a historical morphology case.
- Rule: a knob fitted on a two-body system means something else in a three-body one (🔆 audit C).

## 3. Numerical controls (methods section)
1. Step-size ladder dt, dt/2, dt/4 with the same detector; "converged" only if the change is below the detector floor.
2. Drag-field precision: Float32 arrays give a non-converging precession floor on ⚡; Float64 (`framePrecision:"double"`) converges to 0.00945°/orbit.
3. Two apsidal detectors (osculating ϖ vs multi-periastron fit) must agree before a value is called physical.
4. Period shortening −0.111%/orbit is discretization work (linear in dt) — reported as a numerical, not physical, effect.
5. Same-value re-confirmation contract for run-state edits (bit-invariance) so that UI round-trips never inject drift.

## 4. Ten negative results (one line each; details per wave in docs/PHYSICS.md)
1. Static 1/r⁴ term is absent from E6′ (a moving probe erases spin dragging outside the reference orbit).
2. A common λ that fixes ⚡ destroys the solar system (🪨 −1.51″/century).
3. ⚡ precession floor was Float32 numerics; after removal the value (0.00945°/orbit) is still 2× the observed 0.00473 — not a reproduction.
4. u is instantaneous (no retardation) — no GW-like propagation; petersGW is an external overlay with a fitted dose.
5. Self-force is zero by construction — a lone spinning body never precesses or radiates.
6. Saturn's Q < Jupiter's Q under the DFM ledger — the "Saturn stronger" thought experiment is not supported.
7. Observed-Q transcription for ⚡ gives η = 1.09×10⁻¹⁶ — gravitomagnetism is invisible at the pulsar separation.
8. E6′ spin transport scales as q and contributes 1e-10 — it is not a spin–orbit term.
9. The tested common-dose and monotone-compactness candidates (f, common λ, compactness Ξ, inertia β) did not meet the joint NS/BH/solar constraints — a Ξ-monotone law breaks the BH first (Ξ_BH = 0.5 > Ξ_NS = 0.16). This is a conditional negative for the tested family, not a no-go theorem for new laws; BH rebuilding is a separate task.
10. Period P and apsidal advance ω̇ cannot be satisfied by the same mass factor f under the current uncorrected E12 (wave 248b): Δϖ_DFM = f × Δϖ_GR to 5 digits on three DNS (1.998/1.998/1.997). Candidate fix under test: λ_PN = 1/f (existing key) with the exact speed of light (wave 249a).

## 5. What the model can show (positive, bounded)
- Calibrated apsidal precession from motion dragging (window-declared; not steady over 118 orbits — audit).
- Trailing m=2 spiral response to rotor dragging in a closed 383-body system (E2 emergence level, not E3).
- Conservation bookkeeping of core events (shed, merge, spin-burst) to 1e-11 relative.

- Review-v8 qualification (wave 250c): the wave-249 inventory contains **285 quantities**; 58 of its 62 "agreement" labels rest on a provisional 1% guide rather than an observational uncertainty test, and the machine gate |y_sim - y_obs| <= 3*sigma_obs + eps_num (eps_num <= 0.3*sigma_obs, taken from the two-step-size difference) currently returns "unassessed" for all 285. Keep provenance and statistical agreement in separate columns.
- A rate derived from advance per periastron interval must be converted with the **radial (periastron-to-periastron) period**; fitting the periastron angle directly against time is clearer still. Recomputing the saved D68 record this way gives **38.126 deg/day** (+0.047% against the declared 38.108, -0.306% against the observed 38.243) instead of 38.892 from the sidereal-period conversion. This is an audit correction, not a new integration.
- The fixed-axis force's earlier shell-spin reaction changed rotational energy with no matching axis work. The declared axis is now treated as an external angular-momentum reservoir, coupled axis dynamics remain unimplemented, and the long-window morphology of the three principle samples must be remeasured after this correction.
- Review-v9 qualification (wave 251, three-review consensus on DECISIONS v9): (i) wording — the rotation-channel drag is small in every stellar/NS binary configuration tested (size-ratio gate (R/(R+d))^q), and what saturates for compact pairs is the translation-channel chi (f≈2 is its consequence); this is a result conditioned on the tested size ratios, q, spins and separations, not a general theorem. (ii) Compact-scale force candidates (chi-gated current coupling, Manev-type −α/r² with α=3μ²/c², Ξ-gated 1/r⁶+1/r⁸) are **design notes measured in one harness**, not a resolution of negative result 10 (P vs ω̇); the Manev coefficient 3 reproduces the weak-field first-order advance by construction and is a reference, not a DFM prediction. (iii) MM: the phase count N=∫ds/λ is invariant under the linear-stretch convention of the thought experiment while the time of flight is not (6 : 8 for L=3, β=0.5); under the ω−V·k boundary convention N is not invariant. Neither channel is a "null prediction"; "not contradicted" and "predicted" are kept apart. (iv) The 3σ machine gate passes 0 of 285 quantities because the CSV carried no σ — an implementation of the gate, not completed observational agreement; σ transcription for the NS and stellar systems is the next step.
- Review-v10 qualification (wave 252, three-review consensus on DECISIONS v10): (i) the 2.000 vs 2.025 split of the DFM/observed periastron-advance ratio (J0737/J1757/J1946 vs B1534+12) is a **non-1PN baseline A0** (the λ_PN=0 advance, +2.81% of the observed rate for B1534, dt-converged, ∝ D0pull², numerical floor for the other three), not a difference in the 1PN coefficient (A_PN/obs = 1.998–2.002 for all four); the earlier "the half-prescription necessarily leaves +1.26%" statement was a wrong decomposition. A0's origin is unresolved and is **not** explained by the equal-mass degree. (ii) The "box universe" claim that an equal-mass pair loses its mutual orbit is **not observed**: the common translation against the background drops out of the relative orbit and the orbital translation of the barycentre is exactly zero at q=1, but the relative orbit persists with the same period and precession. (iii) A fixed-wavelength interferometer separates **fringe count ΔN** (insensitive to the arrival-time difference; zero under the stretch convention) from **phase Δφ = −2πν₀(τ₁−τ₂)+ψ** (sensitive to it: δ(Δτ)=10⁻¹⁶ s moves 0.06 fringe); the stretch convention is inconsistent with passive-mirror boundary frequencies under ω=c|k| (ratios (1∓β)²) and remains an assumption. (iv) Periastron substeps are a numerical setting only: the default-dt residual of the compact-force variants is discretization for the eccentric systems (ε_num up to 10% of the observed rate) and the two prescriptions (λ_PN=1/f vs mass-current coupling) cannot be ranked at the default dt; the periastron-interval window is fixed at the first 20 periastra.
- Review-v11 qualification (wave 253, three-review consensus on DECISIONS v11): (i) the "space mesh" whose vertices are the particles themselves (one body: rigid translation+rotation; two bodies: orientation-preserving similarity about the midpoint and the joining segment; three: affine cell), blended with the existing weighted field by χ=W/(W+D₀), is implemented as a **kinematic diagnostic only** — no force, no light and no background coupling are attached, and the default path is bit-identical. (ii) In mesh coordinates the relative orbit freezes (10⁻¹⁶), but in the inertial frame of the same run the period, precession and acceleration are unchanged; the freezing does not require equal masses (unequal pairs freeze identically), so "the orbit disappears for equal masses" is a coordinate statement, not a dynamical one. (iii) The non-1PN baseline A₀ responds quadratically to the background coefficient in all four NS systems as a **difference response** (ratio 4.00 where above the numerical floor), but its magnitude is not (1−χ)² (the implied constant varies tenfold across systems) and its origin remains unidentified; the two-level dt difference is a sensitivity diagnostic, not an error bound, so no quantity currently passes the 3σ gate. (iv) A fixed-wavelength interferometer does see a change of delay difference as a **detector fringe shift** δΔφ/2π; only the frequency-sweep contribution vanishes at Δν=0, and the path wave count under the stretch convention is not a detector null. Stretch is retained as an assumption but is inconsistent with ω=u·k+c|k| plus conservation of ω−V·k at a passive mirror.
- Review-v12 qualification (wave 254, three-review consensus on DECISIONS v12): (i) the space mesh was attached to the dynamics in the agreed order — a full-term coordinate transformation as a **reference test** (round trip at 0.5–0.6 ulp, RK4 order 4.00 in mesh coordinates), then an **opt-in physics candidate** only. The "gravity from the gradient of the mesh" channel reproduces the existing 1/r force bit-for-bit in its kernel (G∇D_grav vs G∇W), so it is a renaming of the gravity already present and is never double-counted. (ii) Both inertia candidates (material derivative and action-form) degenerate at the mesh vertices to χ times the Newtonian acceleration and are therefore **not adopted**; adding them double-counts gravity. (iii) The non-1PN baseline A₀ is **unchanged** by the mesh (printed digits identical with the gravity channel on; inertia candidates inflate it 32–919×), so neither "A₀ originates in the mesh" nor "a mesh lock removes A₀" is claimed. (iv) For galaxies a single global similarity mesh is rejected (differential rotation); a local field with χ(r)→1 at the centre is implemented **display-only**, and the measured v_φ−u_mesh,φ is not zero, so galactic rotation is not yet "riding on the mesh" and the winding of material lines is kinematic, not a rotation-curve explanation. Also: the DFM double-pulsar preset now runs with double-precision frame accumulation by default (Δϖ = +0.009757°/orbit, dt-converged, still ≈2× the observed rate), and the earlier χ≈0.92 statement for Pluto–Charon is withdrawn (χ₁=0.001347, χ₂=0.010961).
- Review-v13 qualification (wave 255, three-review consensus on DECISIONS v13): (i) The mesh enters the dynamics only as a **pair-wise weave** (the E6′ drag relative velocity is read against the instantaneous pair mesh u = χu_node + (1−χ)u_bg; no inertial acceleration is added and the 1/r force is untouched). With the weave on, the momentum ledger closes to the bit and the angular-momentum ledger to 3×10⁻⁷; the drag-induced precession at the χ=1 pole drops by four orders of magnitude, but that pole is the clamp band of the unweaved run and the intermediate band moves the other way, so "a mesh lock removes A₀" is not claimed. Substituting the frame velocity outright reproduces the inertia degeneracy (rejected control). (ii) A finite two-rotor exchange (orbital L, independent mesh J, heat Q≥0) conserves L+J and E+Q exactly and stops at synchronism; Q is heat, not gravitational radiation, and the halo-like reference α(r)=1−χ returns to Keplerian at large r, so neither gravitational-wave emission nor a flat rotation curve is claimed. (iii) For galaxies a local affine reconstruction closes the central hole in v_φ−u_mesh,φ (4.7→1.1) but the outer residual moves by 9% only, because there χ≈0.03 and u≈u_bg: the outer discrepancy is a property of χ and the background, not of the velocity weighting. (iv) All four DFM neutron-star binaries pass the three-step dt gate with positive observed order (ratio 2.001–2.026 to the observed rate at h/4), yet no quantity enters the 3σ observational gate: detector spread and the numerical error budget exceed the observational σ by 10²–10⁹. The Pluto–Charon period excess is 100% in the translational drag channel and is not Galilean-invariant (the background D₀ selects a rest frame). Display: space lines cast from the bodies and the barycentre through a frozen field are a visualization, not photons or material lines; the binary origin is now the barycentre (bit-identical for equal masses).
- Review-v14 qualification (wave 256, three-review consensus on DECISIONS v14): (i) The pair-wise weave was extended into the 1PN band by evaluating ∇u and ∂ₜu of the χ-blended field consistently and substituting only the argument of the velocity-dependent 1PN terms. **It does not replace the mass calibration**: with observed masses (f=1) and no weave all four DFM neutron-star binaries lose their orbit; with the weave the orbit survives but the periastron period shifts by 0.3–1.3% and the precession ratio to observation moves to 3.9–108, away from the calibrated 2.00. The non-1PN baseline A₀ of the calibrated runs vanishes with dt (3.7×10⁻⁶ at h/4), so the calibrated precession is entirely 1PN. (ii) A synthetic-orbit test shows the two periastron detectors agree to 4×10⁻⁸ on clean orbits; their disagreement on simulator output is orbit-side noise (10⁻¹⁰–10⁻⁸ relative), not a definitional difference. With the three-step dt gate applied to the stellar binaries, the observed-mass reproductions of α Cen and Sirius pass the 3σ gate (0.10σ, 0.27σ) while the DFM versions fail it (26σ, 210σ) with the numerical budget satisfied — a first genuine pass/fail from the calibration gate. (iii) For galaxies the drag-limit radius reduces to a threshold quantity s=1−χ(R) independent of the bulk velocity; the outer disc of the principle sample is not entrained under the current χ. The local force needed to hold a spiral arm is ε≈0.06–0.25 of v_c² (Q_T 0.13–0.51); no self-generated arm is claimed. (iv) A cluster with several central black holes expands faster than one with a single or no black hole in all seeds; "stabilize with multiple black holes" is not supported. Display: space lines are the exclusive default, drawn in amber and zoom-invariant; a release candidate is named "real calibration up to neutron-star binaries (numerical convergence)" and explicitly does not claim observational agreement.
